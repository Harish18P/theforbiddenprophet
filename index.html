⚡ Teaser — Watch Before The Ministry Bans It

Welcome, seeker of truth, to The Forbidden Prophet — the publication the Ministry of Magic does not want you to read. Born in the shadows of the wizarding world, we deal not in fairy tales but in the hard currency of power: assets, alliances, and the trades that reshape entire universes. Within these pages you shall find revelations of our most sacred relics, our darkest secrets, and the bold transactions that have cemented Wizarding World's dominance on the inter-universe stage. Tread carefully. What you learn here cannot be unlearned.
